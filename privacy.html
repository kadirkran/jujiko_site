Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy describes how Jujiko ("the app", "we") handles information. Jujiko is developed by Kadir Kıran.

Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contact details through the app. Game progress (levels, coins, gems, lives, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app may show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), IP address, and app interaction data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where required, you will be asked for consent (App Tracking Transparency on iOS, and a consent form in the EEA/UK). You can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings. See how Google uses data.

In-app purchases

Any purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Network access

The app checks whether an internet connection is available in order to show ads and enable online features.

Children

Jujiko is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Because data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change accordingly.
