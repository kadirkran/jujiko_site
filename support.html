Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?

Progress, coins, gems and lives are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app or resetting the device erases them.

Can I play offline?

Levels play offline, but some features such as rewarded ads and purchases need an internet connection.

I have a problem with a purchase.

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, or contact us with your order details.

What should I include in a bug report?

Your device model, iOS version, the level number and a short description of what happened.
